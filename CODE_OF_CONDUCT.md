# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a place where anyone can ask a question, report a problem or propose a change without
being made to feel unwelcome — whatever their experience, background or identity.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and patient. Many people here are learning, and many write in a second language.
- Criticise ideas and code, not people.
- Give feedback you would be glad to receive: specific, kind and useful.
- Accept that maintainers may decline a proposal, and say why when you decline someone else's.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal and political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Promoting paid signals, trading services or other advertising in issues and discussions.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may block
people who keep breaking them.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer privately through the
[GitHub profile](https://github.com/DmytroPalahin). Reports are handled confidentially.
